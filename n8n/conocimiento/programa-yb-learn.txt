YB LEARN - THE YB BROKER CERTIFICATION EXPERIENCE

Source: the YB Learn page of the YB University website (/learn).

NAME
The YB Broker Certification Experience. Program title: Yacht Brokerage Professional Foundations. Tagline: "Your first step into yacht brokerage."

WHAT IT IS
Yacht brokerage is more than knowing boats. It's understanding people, vessels, markets, marketing, negotiation, and transactions. This experience introduces the professional foundations of the business and challenges participants to apply what they learn.

STRUCTURE
16 modules, organized in four phases. Each module combines focused lessons, real-world examples, and practical applications.
- Phase 1, Know the Industry (modules 1-4): yachts, systems, safety, brokerage.
- Phase 2, Find the Opportunity (modules 5-8): prospecting, clients, valuation, listings.
- Phase 3, Build the Business (modules 9-12): marketing, showings, negotiation, transactions.
- Phase 4, Become the Professional (modules 13-16): compliance, networks, client experience, business development.

WHAT PARTICIPANTS LEARN
- Understand the yacht brokerage industry.
- Identify vessel types, systems, and specifications.
- Evaluate market positioning and yacht pricing.
- Develop buyer and seller relationships.
- Create professional yacht listings and marketing strategies.
- Understand negotiation and transaction processes.
- Build a professional approach to brokerage.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- 16 professional learning modules
- Practical assignments after each module
- Competency assessments
- A final brokerage challenge
- The YB University credential in Yacht Brokerage Professional Foundations, on meeting the assessment requirements

FINAL CHALLENGE
Analyze a yacht. Understand its market. Identify a potential buyer. Develop a marketing strategy. Present the vessel. Work through a negotiation scenario. Participants demonstrate their understanding of the brokerage process from opportunity to transaction.

CREDENTIAL
Participants who successfully complete the program and meet its assessment requirements receive the YB University credential in Yacht Brokerage Professional Foundations. This is a YB University-issued educational credential. It is not a government-issued brokerage license or a substitute for any licensing requirements that may apply.

It does not guarantee employment, income, commissions, internships, access to yachts, a brokerage license, academic credit, or any institutional partnership.
